## Structural Principles Expressed In The Archive

The broader archive reinforces the presentation direction without always
linking to a visual example:

- make readability and shareability basic product functions
- let visual organization carry meaning
- preserve parallel lines and cross-sections of thought
- support document-like depth with layered tools
- reveal detail on demand without overwhelming the initial view
- connect intelligence to everyday experience without requiring another
  complicated interface

See [`visual-structure-pass.md`](visual-structure-pass.md) for the concrete
layout analysis and recommended combined structure.
